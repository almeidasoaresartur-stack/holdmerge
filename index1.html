How it works

1

Hold

New orders wait a short time that you choose (for example 30 minutes) before they're released to fulfillment, so a second order has time to arrive.

2

Suggest

When the same customer orders again to the same address, HoldMerge flags the orders and shows what combining them would save.

3

You approve

Nothing is combined until you click Approve. Change your mind? Undo it with one click.

Built to be safe by default

  • Never issues refunds and never charges your customer.
  • Never changes inventory or stock levels.
  • Your original orders stay intact, so your sales and tax reports stay accurate.
  • Nothing is merged automatically unless you turn that on.
  • One-click undo on every action, with a clear log of who did what.

FAQ

Does Shopify already combine orders?

Shopify lets you edit orders by hand. But it doesn't spot repeat orders from the same customer for you, hold them, and let you combine them with one click. That's the gap HoldMerge fills.

Will it change my customer's payment?

No. HoldMerge never refunds and never charges. Each customer payment stays exactly as it was.

Will it delay my shipping?

Only by the hold time you set, and you can set it to zero for any product or order tag.

Does it work with my 3PL or shipping app?

HoldMerge works inside Shopify's own fulfillment flow. We'll confirm compatibility with common apps before launch, and early-access members can tell us which ones they use.
